RAGNA Studio has a built-in email client for Gmail and Outlook. You read, search, reply and organize mail. RAGNA sorts new mail into categories. An agent of your choice can write reply drafts for you. RAGNA never sends anything without your review.

Each user connects one mailbox. Other people in your workspace cannot see it.

<Aside type="note">
  On a self-hosted instance the mail connection needs Google or Microsoft OAuth with mail scopes.
  See [Optional providers](/self-hosting/optional-providers).
</Aside>

## Mail lives in your private workspace

Your mailbox, its drafts and attachments belong to your private workspace. The **Mail** page only works while that workspace is active. In any other workspace you see **Mail lives in your private workspace** with a **Switch to your private workspace** button. RAGNA does not switch for you.

Draft agents and attachments from the media library also come from your private workspace only. To use an agent or a file from a shared workspace, copy it to your private workspace first. See [Workspaces](/workspaces).

## Connect your mailbox

1. Open **Mail**.
2. Choose **Connect Gmail** or **Connect Outlook**.
3. Sign in at Google or Microsoft and grant the requested access.
4. You return to RAGNA Studio. It shows **Syncing your mailbox…** while it imports your most recent 50 inbox threads.

Older mail is not imported up front. When you open an older thread, RAGNA loads it from your provider.

To disconnect, open **Email settings**, go to **General** and choose **Disconnect mailbox**. This removes the synced mail, categories and drafts from RAGNA. Your Gmail or Outlook account itself is not changed.

Disconnecting also revokes the Google access you granted to RAGNA Studio and deletes the stored tokens. This applies when a member is removed from an organization or leaves it, too. Microsoft offers no revocation for apps. RAGNA Studio deletes its stored Microsoft tokens, but a Microsoft administrator who wants to end the grant fully revokes it in Entra under **Enterprise applications**.

## Keep the connection alive

RAGNA checks your mailbox for changes every 5 minutes by default. Use **Sync now** (the refresh button at the bottom of the sidebar) to check right away. The sidebar shows the status: **Syncing...**, **Synced** with a time, or **Sync error**.

Sometimes your provider revokes access, for example after a password change. The status then reads **Reconnect needed**, and RAGNA shows a toast that your mailbox access expired. To fix it:

1. Open **Email settings** and go to **General**.
2. Choose **Reconnect Gmail** or **Reconnect Outlook**.
3. Grant access again.

Syncing resumes. Your categories and settings stay. If connecting fails on a self-hosted instance, see [Troubleshooting](/self-hosting/troubleshooting).

## Read and organize mail

The sidebar lists these folders: **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Sent**, **Archive**, **Trash** and **Drafts**. Below them you find your **Categories** and your provider's **Labels**. Labels are read-only. You can filter by them, but not edit them.

Select a thread to read it. In the thread you can:

- **Star** it, toggle read or unread, **Archive** it, or **Move to trash**. In Trash, the same button reads **Restore from trash**.
- Download attachments with **Download**.

Changes you make here are also made in your Gmail or Outlook mailbox.

### Search and filter

Type in **Search mail**. The search uses your provider's own syntax. For Gmail, try `from:name`, `is:unread` or `has:attachment`. For Outlook, try `from:name` or `hasattachments:true`. Click the help icon next to the field for a reminder.

Above the list you can filter by **Unread**, **Starred** and **Date range**.

### Move many threads to trash

Tick the checkbox on threads, or use **Select all** for every loaded thread. Then choose **Move to trash** and confirm. You can select up to 50 threads at once. You can restore them later from **Trash**.

## Write mail

Choose **Compose** for a new message. On a message, choose **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**.

- Fields: **To**, **Subject**, and **Add Cc/Bcc** for **Cc** and **Bcc**.
- The editor supports rich text. Reply and forward include the quoted history below your text.
- Attach files with **Attach files** or **Attach from library**. Attachments may total 25 MB at most.
- Choose **Send** when you are done.

A thread holds one unsent draft at a time. If you start a different kind of draft (for example a forward while a reply is open), RAGNA asks whether to discard the current one.

Your drafts appear in the **Drafts** folder. The badge shows how many need your attention. RAGNA saves them as you type. Drafts live in RAGNA only. They do not appear in the Drafts folder of Gmail or Outlook.

## Categories

RAGNA sorts new incoming mail into categories with a small AI model. New accounts start with five categories: **To Respond**, **FYI**, **Newsletters**, **Marketing** and **Notifications**. Click a category in the sidebar to see its mail.

Only recent mail is classified. By default that means mail less than one day old, so a first sync does not label your whole inbox.

To manage categories, open **Email settings** and go to **Categories**.

1. Give the category a name and a color.
2. Write a description of what belongs in it. The AI reads this text as written, so be specific.
3. Switch **Auto-draft** on if replies in this category should get a draft (see below).

Deleting a category keeps the history of messages in it, just without a category.

## Draft replies with an agent

An agent can write a reply for you. The result is a draft that you review, edit and send yourself.

### Draft on demand

Open a thread, choose **Draft with AI**, pick an agent under **Choose an agent** and choose **Generate draft**. The agent is preselected from your default draft agent. While it works, the thread shows **Drafting a reply...**.

### Draft automatically

RAGNA drafts a reply on its own in two cases:

- The message falls into a category with **Auto-draft** on.
- The sender is on your auto-draft list. Open **Email settings**, go to **Auto-draft senders** and add the address. Mail from these senders gets a draft regardless of its category.

### Choose the default agent

In **Email settings** under **General**, set **Default draft agent**. It is used for automatic drafts and preselected for **Draft with AI**. The list shows agents of your private workspace. If you pick none, RAGNA uses the default agent of your private workspace. See [Agents](/agents) to create one.
