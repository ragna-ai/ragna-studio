A workspace holds your agents, chats, documents, datasets, tasks, workflows and media. Every item lives in exactly one workspace. Switch workspaces in the switcher at the top of the sidebar.

## Workspace types

Every workspace has a type. You choose it when you create the workspace.

| Type             | Who can open it                                             |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal**     | Only you. Every user gets one named "Private" at sign-up.   |
| **Organization** | Everyone in your organization.                              |
| **Restricted**   | Its workspace members, plus organization owners and admins. |

The switcher groups workspaces by type. Restricted workspaces show a lock icon.

## Workspace roles

Workspace members have one of two roles.

- **Manager:** renames and deletes the workspace and manages its members.
- **Editor:** works with the content of the workspace.

You become manager of every workspace you create. Organization owners and admins can manage every workspace that is not personal.

A personal workspace has no members and cannot be deleted. Organization workspaces list managers only, because everyone else already has access.

## Create a workspace

1. Open the switcher and choose **Manage workspaces**.
2. Choose **New workspace**.
3. Enter a name and pick the visibility. Restricted is preselected.
4. For a restricted workspace, tick the organization members who should get access. They join as editors.

If you have no workspace yet, the app shows a **Create a workspace** button instead of its pages.

## Manage workspace members

Open **Manage workspaces** and choose the members icon next to a workspace. Managers can:

- add members and pick their role,
- change a role between Manager and Editor,
- remove members.

Choose **Leave workspace** to remove yourself.

Organization owners and admins also see all restricted workspaces under **Settings, Organization, Restricted workspaces**. To open one you are not in, join it as manager first.

## Scheduled workflows

A scheduled workflow runs as its author. If the author can no longer open the workspace, or their account is removed, the schedule pauses. The workflow shows a **Schedule paused** badge. The schedule resumes when the author can run it again. Manual runs still work.

## Self-hosting

Workspace types need no setup. They apply to every organization on your instance after you upgrade.
