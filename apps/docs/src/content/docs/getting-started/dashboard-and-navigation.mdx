The app has one navigation sidebar on the left. Everything you open in it belongs to the workspace you have selected at the top of the sidebar.

## The sidebar

The workspace switcher sits at the top. Pick a workspace there to change what every page shows. See [Workspaces](/workspaces).

Below it, the sidebar links to every area of the app:

| Item          | What you find there                                                                              |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Mail**      | Your connected mailbox. Shown only in your personal workspace. See [Email](/integrations/email). |
| **Dashboard** | The overview of the current workspace.                                                           |
| **Tasks**     | The task board. See [Tasks](/content/tasks).                                                     |
| **Workflows** | Automations built from agents. See [Workflows](/workflows).                                      |
| **Agents**    | Your AI co-workers. See [Create an agent](/agents).                                              |
| **Chat**      | Conversations with your agents. See [Chat](/agents/chat).                                        |
| **Datasets**  | Tables you and your agents share. See [Datasets](/content/datasets).                             |
| **Docs**      | Documents and folders. See [Documents](/content/documents).                                      |
| **Images**    | Image generation. See [Images](/media/images).                                                   |
| **Videos**    | Video generation. See [Videos](/media/videos).                                                   |
| **Social**    | Social media posts. See [Social posts](/integrations/social-posts).                              |

The bell and your account menu sit at the bottom of the sidebar.

## The dashboard

The **Dashboard** greets you and shows the latest activity of the current workspace in cards:

- **Tasks**: open tasks, grouped by status.
- **Documents**, **Workflows** and **Chats**: the most recently changed items.
- **Calendar**: tasks that are due this week. Page through the weeks with the arrows.
- **Agents**: your agents, one tile each.

Every card links to its full page with **View all**. An empty card offers a shortcut such as **New task** or **New chat**.

## Notifications

The bell shows a red dot when you have unread notifications. You get one when:

- a workflow run completes or fails,
- a task reminder is due,
- generated images or a generated video are ready, or failed.

Choose a notification to open what it is about. If it belongs to another workspace, the app switches there first. In the panel you can mark all as read, delete one notification, or clear all of them.

## Your account menu

Choose your initials at the bottom of the sidebar to open the account menu:

- **Account** and **Settings** open your account page.
- **Organization** opens the organization settings. See [Organizations](/workspaces/organizations).
- **MCP** opens the MCP connector settings. It shows only when the connector is enabled. See [MCP connector](/integrations/mcp).
- **Sign out** ends your session.

The account page has these tabs:

| Tab                    | What you set                                                 |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Profile**            | Your name and avatar.                                        |
| **Connected accounts** | Connect or disconnect Google, Microsoft and LinkedIn.        |
| **Language**           | English or Deutsch.                                          |
| **Sidebar**            | **Show labels when collapsed** keeps the item names visible. |
| **Delete account**     | Deletes your account.                                        |
