An organization groups the people who work together. It owns its workspaces. Everyone you invite joins the same organization. Open its settings from the user menu with **Organization**.

## Organization roles

| Role       | What it can do                                                                                                              |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner**  | Everything an organization admin can do. Also transfers ownership and deletes the organization. There is one owner.         |
| **Admin**  | Invites and removes members, changes member roles, renames the organization, opens every non-personal workspace as manager. |
| **Member** | Works in the workspaces they can open and creates new ones. Sees the member list but cannot change it.                      |

These roles differ from the [workspace roles](/workspaces/members) manager and editor.

## Invite someone

Organization owners and admins invite people.

1. Open **Organization** from the user menu.
2. In **Invitations**, choose **Invite member**.
3. Enter the **Email address** and pick a **Role**: **Member** or **Admin**.
4. Choose **Send invitation**.

The person gets an email. They join your organization when they sign up with this email address. The invitation is valid for 48 hours.

Pending invitations show their role and expiry date. Choose **Copy link** to share the invitation yourself, or the cross icon (**Cancel invitation**) to withdraw it.

Some invitations are rejected:

- The person already has an account.
- The person was removed from your organization. Restore them in the member list instead.
- On a self-hosted instance, the email is not on the list of allowed sign-in emails.

You cannot invite someone as owner. Use ownership transfer instead.

## Manage members

The **Members** card lists everyone in the organization. Owners and admins can:

- change a role between **Member** and **Admin** in the role menu,
- remove a member with the trash icon.

A removed member is signed out and loses access right away. The member list marks them **Removed** and shows a **Deleted on** date. Choose **Restore** to bring them back before that date. After 30 days their account and personal data are deleted for good.

You cannot remove the owner.

## Organization name

Owners and admins can rename the organization in the **Organization name** card.

## Restricted workspaces

Restricted workspaces are private to their members, but owners and admins keep oversight. The **Restricted workspaces** card lists every restricted workspace with its number of workspace members.

- If you are a member, the row shows **You are a member** and an **Open** button.
- If you are not, choose **Join as manager**. You then appear as a workspace manager in that workspace and can open it from the switcher.

## Transfer ownership

Only the owner sees **Transfer ownership**.

1. Choose a member under **Choose a member**.
2. Choose **Transfer ownership** and confirm.

The new owner takes over. You become an organization admin. You cannot undo this yourself.

## Delete the organization

Only the owner can delete the organization under **Danger zone**.

1. Choose **Delete organization**.
2. Type the organization name to confirm.

All members are signed out and lose access right away. You have 30 days to restore the organization. After that, all its data is deleted for good.

During those 30 days everyone who signs in sees "is scheduled for deletion" with the final date. Only the owner sees **Restore organization**.

## Leave the organization

Members and admins leave by deleting their own account. Open your account settings, go to the **Delete account** tab and choose **Delete my account**. You are signed out right away. Your organization can restore your account for 30 days. After that, your account and personal data are deleted for good.

The owner cannot leave. Transfer ownership or delete the organization first.
