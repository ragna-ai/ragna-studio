Each workspace has its own members and roles. Access to a workspace depends on its [type](/workspaces) and on your role in the organization.

## Workspace roles

| Role        | What it can do                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manager** | Renames and deletes the workspace and manages its members.          |
| **Editor**  | Works with the content of the workspace. Cannot manage its members. |

You become manager of every workspace you create. A workspace can have several managers.

## Who has which role

| Workspace type   | Roles                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Personal**     | Only you, as manager. It has no member list and you cannot delete it.                                              |
| **Organization** | Every organization member works as an editor. Only managers are listed. Add a manager to give someone more rights. |
| **Restricted**   | Only the listed workspace members. Each one is a manager or an editor.                                             |

Organization owners and admins act as managers in every workspace that is not personal. This holds even if they are not listed as workspace members.

## Add members

Only workspace managers can add members.

1. Open the switcher and choose **Manage workspaces**.
2. Choose the members icon (**Workspace members**) next to the workspace.
3. Pick people under **Add workspace members**. For a restricted workspace, also pick a role: **Manager** or **Editor**.
4. Choose **Add**.

You can only add active members of your organization. To bring in someone new, [invite them to the organization](/workspaces/organizations) first.

In an organization workspace the panel is called **Managers** and you can only add managers.

## Change a role or remove a member

In a restricted workspace, managers change a member's role in the role menu next to their name. Choose the trash icon to remove a member. They can no longer open the workspace.

The role of members in an organization workspace cannot change, because everyone there is an editor already. To take away a manager role, remove that manager from the list.

## Leave a workspace

If you are listed as a workspace member, choose **Leave workspace** at the bottom of the members panel. Confirm the dialog. You can only open the workspace again if a manager adds you.

Organization owners and admins keep access to every restricted workspace, even after they leave one.

## Personal workspace

Your personal workspace is called "Private". Nobody else can open it, not even organization owners and admins. It has no member list and you cannot delete it. Your [email](/integrations/email) inbox, drafts and attachments live there.
