Each workspace has its own members and roles. Access to a workspace depends on its [type](/workspaces) and on the user's role in the organization.

## Workspace roles

| Role        | What it can do                                                      |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manager** | Renames and deletes the workspace and manages its members.          |
| **Editor**  | Works with the content of the workspace. Cannot manage its members. |

The creator becomes manager of every workspace they create. A workspace can have several managers.

## Who has which role

| Workspace type   | Roles                                                                                                             |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Personal**     | Only its owner, as manager. It has no member list and cannot be deleted.                                          |
| **Organization** | Every organization member works as an editor. Only managers are listed. Add a manager to give a user more rights. |
| **Restricted**   | Only the listed workspace members. Each one is a manager or an editor.                                            |

Organization owners and admins act as managers in every workspace that is not personal. This holds even if they are not listed as workspace members.

## Add members

Only workspace managers can add members.

1. Open the switcher and choose **Manage workspaces**.
2. Choose the members icon (**Workspace members**) next to the workspace.
3. Pick users under **Add workspace members**. For a restricted workspace, also pick a role: **Manager** or **Editor**.
4. Choose **Add**.

Only active members of the organization can be added. To bring in a new user, [invite them to the organization](/workspaces/organizations) first.

In an organization workspace the panel is called **Managers** and only managers can be added.

## Change a role or remove a member

In a restricted workspace, managers change a member's role in the role menu next to their name. Choose the trash icon to remove a member. They can no longer open the workspace.

The role of members in an organization workspace cannot change, because every user there is an editor already. To take away a manager role, remove that manager from the list.

## Leave a workspace

A listed workspace member can choose **Leave workspace** at the bottom of the members panel and confirm the dialog. The workspace opens again only if a manager adds the user back.

Organization owners and admins keep access to every restricted workspace, even after they leave one.

## Personal workspace

The personal workspace is called "Private". No other user can open it, not even organization owners and admins. It has no member list and cannot be deleted. The user's [email](/integrations/email) inbox, drafts and attachments live there.
